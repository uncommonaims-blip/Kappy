# Kappy product direction (draft)

## Mission
Make caregiving more personal, connected, and dependable by bringing families, caregivers, and trusted communities together.

## Primary users
- Care recipients and their authorized representatives
- Professional and informal caregivers
- Family members
- Care-circle coordinators

## MVP hypotheses to validate
1. Shared shifts and coverage requests reduce scheduling friction.
2. A concise handoff feed improves continuity without overwhelming teams.
3. Daily routines and checklists help coordinate non-clinical support.
4. Clear roles and invitations make participation manageable.

## Proposed MVP
- Authentication and invitation-only care circles
- Role-based permissions and membership management
- Shift calendar, availability, and coverage requests
- Care notes and daily task lists
- Basic caregiver profiles

## Future concepts (not committed)
- Caregiver matching and compatibility
- Vetting and training
- Community companionship and intergenerational programs
- Payments, billing, and marketplace features

## Open ideation questions
- Who creates and owns a circle?
- What information should a care recipient control?
- What is the minimum viable shift-swap flow?
- Which notes need structured fields versus free text?
- What should family members see versus caregivers?
- Is Kappy initially a team tool or a caregiver marketplace?

Kappy is a temporary name. No naming or trademark clearance is implied.
